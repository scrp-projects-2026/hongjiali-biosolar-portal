HEAVY FREIGHT FOCUS // SECTION 02

Commercial Mobility & Freight Corridors

Ready

In South Africa, electric mobility cannot rely on an unstable municipal electrical grid. Bio-Solar microgrids create off-grid energy nodes every 150-200 km along vital transport routes, enabling continuous 200kW–720kW charging for heavy electric trucks.

N3 Corridor Durban ↔ Johannesburg

Primary freight highway carrying 12,000+ diesel trucks daily. Microgrids utilize agricultural swine/poultry residues for megawatt charging.

Target HW Charging: 720kW Liquid-Cooled
N1 Highway JHB ↔ Cape Town

1,400 km long-distance logistics artery. Combines solar PV, biogas, and Hongjiali BESS to support battery swap stations for fleet logistics.

Key Infra: Battery Swap + BESS
N4 Mining Belt Pretoria ↔ Maputo Port

Heavy mining transport route requiring continuous 24/7 power for heavy electric haulers and zero-emission ore transportation.

Power Demand: 2MW Continuous Grid
